Features

Everything to go from pixels to scenarios.

EXTRACT

Scenario extraction

Depth models reconstruct each scene in 3D and pull driving scenarios straight out of raw video — no manual authoring.

TAG

Scenario tagging

Automatic tags for driving context and environment, so your team finds rare, critical edge cases across petabytes of data.

ENRICH

Scenario enrichment

Spin countless variations from a single event by changing key parameters — expand your library with minimal effort.

EXPORT

Scenario export

Export to the industry-standard ASAM OpenSCENARIO format for easy simulator integration — custom formats on request.
