GenixCore

Mine edge-case scenarios out of driving video.

GenixCore reads ordinary footage in three dimensions — reconstructing the environment, recovering ego-motion, and tracking every actor — then hands you a clean, taggable scenario ready for your validation tool-chain.

GenixSim

A hyper-real testbed rebuilt from your own data.

GenixSim reconstructs a 4D world from recorded drives and lets your full stack drive inside it — photoreal, reactive, and closed-loop. Step into the exact moment an edge case happened and replay it a thousand ways.

  • 4D world reconstruction from driving data
  • Photorealistic sensor synthesis, ready to feed your models
  • Closed-loop testing for E2E & VLA driving stacks
  • Interactive scenario & environment configuration

Under the hood

The reconstruction engine.

3D / 4D

Volumetric reconstruction

High-fidelity, navigable replicas of real scenes from standard video — geometry, appearance, and motion over time.

TRACK

Dynamic object recovery

Camera pose, trajectories, lanes, and per-actor 3D detection, classification, and tracking through the whole sequence.

RENDER

Neural sensor synthesis

Photo-realistic camera views generated on demand, controllable down to actors, environment, and sensor parameters.

LOOP

Closed-loop validation

Open-loop replay on recorded data and closed-loop testing in the reconstructed world — without a single risky real-vehicle run.

Where it runs
