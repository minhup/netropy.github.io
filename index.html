Extract · Scenario

GenixScenario

Formerly GenixCore

Mine edge-case scenarios out of any driving video.

GenixScenario reads ordinary footage in three dimensions — reconstructing the environment, recovering ego-motion, and tracking every actor — then hands you a clean, taggable scenario ready for your validation tool-chain.

  • 3D environment reconstruction from camera-only input
  • Dynamic object detection, classification & tracking
  • Searchable scenario metadata & tagging
  • Export to ASAM OpenSCENARIO & OpenDRIVE for any simulator

Reconstruct

GenixRecon

Turn a logged drive into a living 3D world.

GenixRecon rebuilds recorded driving logs as a dynamic 3D Gaussian-splat world — geometry, appearance, and every moving actor, faithfully recovered over time. The result drops straight into GenixSim, ready to re-render from any viewpoint.

  • Dynamic 3D Gaussian splatting from raw driving logs
  • Full scene and per-actor motion recovered over time
  • Free-viewpoint, photoreal neural rendering
  • Sim-ready output that hands off directly to GenixSim

Simulate

GenixSim

Edit the world. Close the loop.

GenixSim turns a reconstructed world into an interactive testbed. Reshape the scene by hand, by config, or by asking an agent — then drive your full stack inside it, closed-loop, on high-fidelity generated sensor data. A simulator built for autonomous driving, not adapted to it.

  • Edit scenarios three ways — manual, config, or LLM agent
  • Closed-loop test, training & validation for E2E / VLA stacks
  • High-fidelity generated camera & sensor streams
  • Focused on the road, where general robotics sims stop short

Generate

GenixRetake

Re-drive the scene. Skip the rebuild.

GenixRetake edits the drive itself. Move the ego vehicle or any surrounding actor onto a new path, and a video model renders the alternate take directly from the original dashcam — no full reconstruction, no hand-labeled scene. The fastest route from “what if” to footage.

  • Re-route ego and surrounding objects on a dashcam clip
  • Direct video generation — no reconstruction step
  • Works without full scene annotation
  • New corner cases delivered as ready-to-use video
