The Netropy Advantage

For embodied AI advancements like autonomous driving, training and validation in virtual environments is essential. Instead of scaling fleets of cars, we aim to make testing more accessible, efficient, and scalable. Our platform generates realistic synthetic data - RGB color, depth, occupancy grid, and semantic information - directly from videos and images, with no need for additional sensors (like LiDAR or GPS), modeling, asset stores, or game engines.

This novel workflow redefines simulation, eliminating traditional, time-consuming processes. It's a glimpse into the future of AI development, where virtual environments become dynamic testing grounds for real-world applications.

  • Accelerate learning with our vast, AI-generated datasets and scenarios.
  • Rigorously test your algorithms across countless virtual situations.
  • Refine your mobile robot's performance in a safe, controllable environment.

Our platform enables rapid iteration, reduces physical testing costs, and dramatically speeds up the development cycle for mobile robotics.
