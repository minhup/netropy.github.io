About Netropy

Netropy is founded by serial entrepreneurs with expertise in robotics and computer vision, who have successfully launched robotics startups. Based on the latest Neural Rendering and Generative AI technologies, we develop realistic and immersive simulation and digital twin solutions for various industries including autonomous driving and robotics.

Having recently completed our funding round, we're poised for rapid growth and looking for talented individuals to join our team in creating innovation together.

Technology Stack

  • Neural Rendering: Creating physically accurate and highly realistic virtual environments through cutting-edge technologies like NeRF and Gaussian Splatting.
  • Visual Generative AI: Our simulation AI models generate diverse scenarios and virtual environments to train and maximize the performance of virtual agents like robots.
  • Closed-Loop Simulation: Providing dynamic simulation environments that respond in real-time to trained agents' actions, enabling reliable testing.
